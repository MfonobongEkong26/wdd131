Serverbase Engineering Limited

Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Serverbase Engineering Limited

This name represents a construction and engineering company. I chose the name because it clearly identifies the company and communicates its focus on engineering, construction, and professional project services.

Proposed domain: serverbaseengineering.com

2. Site Purpose

The website will introduce Serverbase Engineering Limited and provide visitors with information about the company's construction and engineering services. The site will showcase selected projects, explain the services offered, provide company information, and give potential clients a convenient way to contact the company about construction projects.

3. Scenarios

These are questions that potential visitors may ask:

  • What construction and engineering services does Serverbase Engineering Limited provide?
  • How can I contact Serverbase Engineering Limited about a construction project?
  • What types of construction projects has the company completed?

4. Color Schema

The website will use a professional color scheme that represents construction, engineering, reliability, and professionalism.

Dark Navy

#1F2937

Used for the header, footer, headings, and major navigation areas.

Construction Orange

#F59E0B

Used for buttons, accents, links, and important highlights.

White

#FFFFFF

Used for the main background and content areas.

5. Typography

Roboto Slab

This font will be used for the site name, main headings, and section headings.

Roboto

This font will be used for paragraphs, navigation, descriptions, buttons, and general website content.
